Ph.D. Candidate in Finance

W. P. Carey School of Business, Arizona State University

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Finance at Arizona State University's W. P. Carey School of Business. My research focuses on empirical corporate finance, valuation, and behavioral finance. My recent work examines: 1) the practice of corporate bond valuation from sell-side debt analysts and 2) the value of labor–management relational quality as a sub-component of corporate culture.
