Biwon Lee

Ph.D. Candidate in Finance

W. P. Carey School of Business, Arizona State University

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Finance at Arizona State University's W. P. Carey School of Business. My research focuses on empirical corporate finance, valuation, and behavioral finance. I hold an M.S. in Finance and a B.A. in French Language and Literature and Business Administration from Korea University. Prior to my doctoral studies, I worked at the Daishin Economic Research Institute in Seoul, where I evaluated ESG scores for listed Korean companies. My recent work examines labor–management relationships and debt valuation.
